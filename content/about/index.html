Profile

I am a dedicated public health researcher and educator with over 15 years of experience in community health and academic research. My work focuses on the epidemiology of infectious diseases, where I apply advanced quantitative methods such as structural equation modelling and Item Response Theory to improve disease surveillance. Whether I am training health workers in Iraq or conducting epidemiological studies in Malaysia, my goal is to bridge the gap between complex data and practical public health solutions.

Research Interests

My research focuses on the design, measurement, and evaluation of community-based infectious disease surveillance. My principal interests include:

  • Infectious disease epidemiology, surveillance, and disease modelling.
  • Community engagement and participation in public health surveillance.
  • Development, cultural adaptation, and validation of public health instruments.
  • Exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis, Item Response Theory, structural equation modelling, and path analysis.
  • Applied epidemiological methods for evidence-informed public health policy.
