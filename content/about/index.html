Profile

I am a public health researcher and educator with more than 15 years of combined experience in community health, academic research, and health-worker training. My work connects epidemiology, biostatistics, psychometrics, and community engagement to strengthen infectious disease surveillance and practical public health decision-making.

Research Interests

My research focuses on the design, measurement, and evaluation of community-based infectious disease surveillance. I am particularly interested in the following areas:

  • Infectious disease epidemiology, surveillance, and disease modelling.
  • Community engagement and participation in public health surveillance.
  • Development, cultural adaptation, and validation of public health instruments.
  • Exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis, Item Response Theory, structural equation modelling, and path analysis.
  • Applied epidemiological methods for evidence-informed public health policy.
